hyperwell

A falling-block puzzle with one more direction. The floor of the well is a whole cube, so a layer is full only when all of its cells are filled, across every step of ana and kata. Pieces turn in planes, a quarter turn at a time. Nothing falls until you drop it, so take your time.

The left view draws the well like a tesseract: cells further toward ana sit in the outer shell, cells toward kata in the inner one. The right view cuts the same well into ordinary 3D slices.
